ABOUT US

Freixo, founded in 2026, is a dynamic Qatar-based company specializing in premium wholesale and retail trading solutions. Built on over a decade of specialized industry experience, Freixo focuses on a multi-channel trading approach, prioritizing high-quality Disposable Solutions, Commercial Cleaning Materials, and Essential Building Materials through wholesale, retail, and long-term contractual supply.

We deliver comprehensive supply chain support across multiple sectors in Qatar, catering directly to large-scale commercial projects, corporate offices, residential compounds, and private homes. From high-grade packaging and disposables to specialized cleaning supplies and construction essentials, we ensure seamless on-site delivery that satisfies both large corporate demands and everyday household needs. Backed by a powerful supplier network, deep market knowledge, and a customer-first approach, Freixo is committed to being the most trusted, one-stop trading partner for projects, offices, and homes across Qatar.
